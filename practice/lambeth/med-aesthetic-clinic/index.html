Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?
Book directly via the clinic's website. As a private provider, no NHS referral or registration is needed, though some specialists may ask for a GP referral letter.
How much does it cost?
Fees are set by the clinic and vary by service. Most private clinics publish prices on their website or provide them on enquiry. Many are recognised by private medical insurers such as Bupa, AXA Health, Aviva and Vitality — check with your insurer before booking.
Is this clinic regulated?
Yes — MED AESTHETIC CLINIC is registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which regulates all providers of medical care in England, private and NHS alike. Registration details and any inspection reports are linked above.
How do I find a named consultant?
The Private Healthcare Information Network (PHIN) is the official, CMA-mandated source of information on individual private consultants in the UK, including fees and activity volumes. Search for consultants at this or any location on phin.org.uk.
