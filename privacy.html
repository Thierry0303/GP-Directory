Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Overview

London GP Directory ("we", "the site", "the app") is a free directory of NHS GP practices, private clinics and dentists in London. This page explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the website or the Android app.

No account, no sign-in

The site and app do not require you to create an account, register, or sign in. There is no user database and no personal profile is stored.

What data is collected

We do not run any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. No cookies are set for tracking purposes. Specifically:

  • Search and postcode lookup: if you search by postcode or use "Near me", that postcode or your device's location (only with your explicit permission, via your browser's location prompt) is sent directly to postcodes.io, a third-party open geocoding service, solely to calculate distances and sort results. This is not stored by us.
  • Practice and clinic data: the directory information itself (NHS GP practices, private clinics, dentists) is sourced from the NHS ODS FHIR API and the Care Quality Commission (CQC), and is public information about healthcare providers — not personal data about you.
  • Standard hosting logs: our hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability purposes, in line with standard web server practices. These logs are not used for tracking or profiling.

Third-party links

The site links out to NHS.uk, CQC.org.uk, GP registration pages, and individual clinics' own websites. Once you follow a link to one of these external sites, their own privacy policy applies — we have no control over and are not responsible for their practices.

Children's privacy

This service is a general reference directory and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about visitors, there is no personal data held about you to access, correct, or delete. If you have any privacy question or concern, you can contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on our About page.
